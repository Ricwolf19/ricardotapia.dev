## Context

ESPAU (Esperanza para el Autismo, I.A.P.) is a nonprofit in Chihuahua dedicated
to the diagnosis and therapy of Autism Spectrum Disorder. The project pairs the
public institutional site with a clinical and administrative back office that
runs the organization's daily work.

## Architecture

The public site presents the organization's six programmes — assessment and
diagnosis with ADOS-2, ADI-R and ABAS-II; individual and group therapy; a DENVER
model preschool; parent guidance; and professional training — alongside
donations, transparency reporting and contact.

The back office covers clinical operations: student records, therapists,
schedules, weekly plans generated automatically by cron, assessments and an
audit log, all under role-based access control. Minors' health data is encrypted
at rest and only released inside a step-up window verified with TOTP.

## Stack

- Next.js with the App Router and TypeScript; Tailwind CSS.
- PostgreSQL with typed queries (pgtyped + zql) and node-pg-migrate migrations.
- Auth.js v5 with TOTP mandatory for staff.
- Admin lists with `@pibytelabs/listkit`; scheduled jobs on Vercel crons.

## Technical challenges

- Protecting minors' health data: encryption at rest with release limited to a
  step-up TOTP window, without getting in the way of daily operations.
- Generating weekly plans automatically by cron from schedules and therapist
  availability.
- A complete audit log of clinical access and changes.

## Outcome

A production platform ([www.espau.com](https://www.espau.com/)) that combines an
institutional presence with a secure, typed clinical back office and
reproducible migrations.
