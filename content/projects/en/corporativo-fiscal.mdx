## Context

CorpFiscal is the internal operating platform of an accounting and tax firm. It
is not a product for sale: it is the system the firm works in every day —
customer portal, reception, HR, case records and invoicing — gathered into one
monorepo around a central API.

## Architecture

A Yarn 4 Workspaces monorepo split into `apps/*` and `shared/*`, with 13
applications:

- `web` — the corporate portal; `customers` — a self-service portal for clients.
- `rh` — digital HR records; `reception`, `materialization`, `accounting`,
  `corporate`, `legal` and `admin` — internal operations, one app per area.
- `landiabar` — a PWA for the in-house snack bar.
- `visionfiscal` — a real-time collaborative whiteboard for tax-planning
  sessions, built with Excalidraw + Yjs + Socket.io.
- `auth` — centralized authentication; `api` — an Express 5 backend on
  MongoDB/Mongoose.

Baseline security: mandatory 2FA/OTP (speakeasy), fine-grained RBAC across the
ADMIN, ACCOUNTANT, CUSTOMER, HR, BARISTA and SERVICE roles, and digital records
with document-level encryption.

## Stack

- Frontends: React 18 + Vite + React Router 7 + Tailwind 4 + TanStack Query.
- Backend: Express 5 + MongoDB/Mongoose, JWT, Socket.io.
- Invoicing: Facturapi (CFDI/SAT) with deep domain coverage — CFDI
  multi-payment, the SAT cancellation cycle and payment CFDIs. Payments: Stripe.
- SAT scraping with Puppeteer; document generation with pdf-lib; image
  processing with Sharp.
- WhatsApp messaging. Storage: Google Cloud Storage. Scheduled jobs: node-cron.

## Technical challenges

- Coordinating 13 frontends against one shared backend while keeping types and
  SAT catalogs consistent.
- Real-time collaboration with CRDTs (Yjs) synchronized over sockets in the
  tax-planning whiteboard.
- Automating tax-status lookups against the SAT with Puppeteer scraping without
  breaking the taxpayer's session.

## Outcome

A modular platform where each area of the firm has its own app, all fed by a
central API with electronic invoicing, encrypted digital records and automated
tax workflows.
